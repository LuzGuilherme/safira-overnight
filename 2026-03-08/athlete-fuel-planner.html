Workout Nutrition

🌅 Pre-Workout (2-3h before)
🍚 80-100g carbs — oatmeal, toast, rice
🥚 15-25g protein — eggs, yogurt, lean meat
⚠️ Low fiber & fat to prevent GI issues
🔥 During Training (>60 min)
⚡ 60-90g carbs/hour — gels, drinks, bars
🧂 500-1000mg sodium/hour in hot conditions
💡 Train your gut — practice race nutrition
🔄 Post-Workout (within 30-60 min)
🍌 1-1.2g/kg carbs — fruit, rice, potatoes
💪 0.3-0.4g/kg protein — whey, chicken, fish
🥛 Recovery drink: chocolate milk or protein shake
📈 Build Phase Tips
🎯 Focus on quality calories to support increased intensity
📊 Periodize carbs: more on hard days, less on easy days
