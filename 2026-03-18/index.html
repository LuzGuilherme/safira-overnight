Profit First Principles

1. Pay Yourself First

Before paying any expenses, allocate to Profit and Owner's Pay. This forces your business to operate on what remains.

2. Use Separate Accounts

Open 4 bank accounts (or sub-accounts): Profit, Owner's Pay, Tax, and Operating Expenses. Transfer on the 1st and 15th.

3. Small Plates Theory

By limiting your OpEx account, you'll find creative ways to operate leaner. Constraints breed innovation.

4. Quarterly Profit Distribution

Every quarter, take 50% of your Profit account as a reward. The other 50% stays as a cash buffer.
