⚡ REALITY CHECK

→ True Fans ≠ Followers. A true fan will buy everything you make. They're your superfans.
→ ~10% conversion rate from casual followers to true fans is optimistic. Plan for 1-5%.
→ Relationship > Reach. 1,000 superfans beats 100,000 passive followers every time.
→ Multiple products help. More offerings = more revenue per fan = fewer fans needed.
→ Direct connection is key. Email lists, Discord, Patreon — own the relationship.
