💡 How to Spot AI Writing

Here are some patterns that often indicate machine-generated text:

  • Perfect structure — AI tends to be overly organized
  • Hedging language — "It's important to note...", "One might argue..."
  • Lack of personal voice — Generic, neutral tone
  • Too comprehensive — Covers all angles equally
  • Unusual word choices — "Delve", "crucial", "landscape"

Human writing often has quirks, incomplete thoughts, strong opinions, and personal references.
