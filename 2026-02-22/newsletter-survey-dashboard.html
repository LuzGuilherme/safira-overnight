Critical Rules

Non-negotiable guidelines from reader feedback.

❌ Never use the word "expat"
Multiple readers explicitly requested this. Use "international residents" or "foreigners" instead. The term implies privilege and is off-putting.
⚠️ Give more advance notice
"Alert to events sooner, allowing to buy tickets in advance" — Include a "Coming Next Week" section for events that require booking.
⚠️ Complete event information
"Festival location, dates, and hours — seems basic but it's hard to find" — Every event MUST have: date, time, location, price.
✅ Include free/budget options
"Address topics accessible to all, not just high end" — Prioritize free events and budget-friendly experiences in every issue.
